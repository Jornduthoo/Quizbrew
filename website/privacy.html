Privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Quizbrew runs on your own laptop, on your own network. There are no accounts, and the app sends nothing about you or your players to us.

  • On the laptop: your quizzes, pictures, settings and scores stay on the laptop (in the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\Quizbrew), or in the cloud folder you pick yourself.
  • Players join with a team name and a colour. That stays on the laptop for the quiz night.
  • Only when you ask for it does Quizbrew go online: AI questions go to the AI service whose key you entered (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI), and picture search asks Wikimedia Commons and Openverse. Their own privacy rules apply.
  • Licence codes are checked on the laptop itself, without internet.
  • This website is hosted by Cloudflare, uses no cookies and no trackers.

Questions: hello@quizbrew.app
