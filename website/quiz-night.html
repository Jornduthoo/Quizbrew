How to organise a quiz night

A good quiz night is mostly planning. This guide takes you from the first idea to the last scoreboard, whether it is for a sports club, a pub, a school, a youth group or a birthday. At the bottom you will find a checklist and 10 free sample questions.

1Pick the date, the venue and the format

  • Set the date at least three weeks ahead. Friday and Saturday evenings work best.
  • Teams of 4 to 6 people feel the most fun. Ask teams to sign up in advance, so you know how many tables you need.
  • Plan 6 to 8 rounds of 8 to 10 questions. With a break halfway, that is an evening of 2.5 to 3 hours.

2Sort out the screen and the sound

  • A TV or projector that every table can see, a laptop, an HDMI cable and speakers for the music round.
  • Test everything in the venue itself, ideally a few days before. A microphone helps from about 15 teams.

With Quizbrew, the laptop makes its own network if the venue has no good wifi. Teams play on their phones, no internet needed.

3Write questions that everyone enjoys

  • Mix topics: general knowledge, music, sport, film, geography, the local area.
  • Start easy and build up. Every team should know a few answers in every round.
  • Avoid questions anyone can look up in two seconds, and check every answer twice.
  • Vary the rounds: a picture round, a music round, a drawing round or a "closest wins" question keeps the room awake.

4Agree the rules and the scoring

  • Say clearly whether phones may be used, and how many points each question is worth.
  • Keep a tiebreaker ready: a "closest wins" number question settles a draw fairly.
  • Show the standings after every round. That is when the room gets loud.

5Raise money (for clubs and schools)

  • Ask an entry fee of €10 to €20 per team, paid when signing up.
  • The bar and snacks during the break often bring in more than the entry fees.
  • Ask local shops for prizes and thank them on the screen between rounds.
  • A raffle during the break adds extra income and fun.

Checklist for the night

  • Laptop and charger
  • HDMI cable (and an adapter if needed)
  • Extension lead and power strip
  • TV or projector tested in the venue
  • Sound tested for the music round
  • Quiz run through once from start to finish
  • Pens and paper for teams without a phone
  • Prizes, and a prize for the last place
  • Sponsor names on a slide
  • A tiebreaker question

10 free quiz questions

Free to use at your own quiz night. They come from the sample quiz that is included in Quizbrew.

  1. Which is the smallest planet in our solar system? Mercury · Mars · Venus · Pluto
  2. How many minutes are there in a day? 1,240 · 1,440 · 1,640 · 1,040
  3. Which gas do plants take in from the air? Oxygen · Carbon dioxide · Nitrogen · Helium
  4. Who painted the Mona Lisa? Michelangelo · Leonardo da Vinci · Raphael · Rembrandt
  5. Which animal is the fastest on land? Lion · Cheetah · Horse · Greyhound
  6. How many players does a football team have on the pitch? 9 · 10 · 11 · 12
  7. Bananas are berries. True · False
  8. You can see the Great Wall of China from the Moon with the naked eye. True · False
  9. How many countries are members of the United Nations? Closest wins
  10. In which year were the first modern Olympic Games held? Closest wins
